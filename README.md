Sum up 24 November's world news in one image: a one-page newspaper front page for Tuesday 24 November 2026 — Ukraine sinking a Russian warship off Libya, Nvidia gaining almost $1 trillion as Jensen Huang is reinstated, COP31 ending with no deal, 24 killed in an Indonesia factory fire, 14 killed in Kyiv region strikes, Alcaraz winning the ATP Finals, and the ICJ ruling Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, plus a ticker with NATO space defence, Denmark's "ghetto law", Trump, Manchester, Modi and the World Chess Championship. The image is drawn by `draw.py` (Pillow) from the day's real reporting, and `main.py` copies it to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

